Problem Statement(s)

  • • Problem: Students face long physical queues at the campus printing and Xerox shop, especially during short breaks and assignment deadlines.
  • • Background: Printing requests are received in person. Staff must collect files, confirm page and copy counts, calculate charges, collect payment and communicate status.
  • • Impact on Students: Break time lost waiting; students risk being late to class or failing to collect prints before sessions.
  • • Impact on Staff: Multiple walk-in requests, manual file handling, and pricing checks create congestion and order mix-ups.
  • • Need: A single campus platform to submit print jobs in advance, obtain a queue token, and collect completed work without waiting beside the counter.

Objectives & Success Criteria

  • • Primary Objective: Develop a web-based campus printing & Xerox ordering system digitizing order submission and queue handling.
  • • Student Functions: Upload supported documents; select page range, copies, color/B&W, and single/double-sided options; view calculated price; submit order.
  • • Shop Functions: View incoming orders, access submitted files, update order status, verify payment, and manage print queue.
  • • Queue & Communication: Generate a unique token for each accepted order and notify students when ready for collection.
  • • Success Measures: Correct price calculation; 100% unique tokens; accurate order-status updates; reduction in observed waiting time.

Literature Review

Review 1
Scope of Review

Virtual queue systems, service-system waiting-time prediction, queue design and digital order workflows. Direct studies of campus Xerox shops are limited.

Virtual Queue Implementation

Basgaran & Ibrahim (2023) describe a web-based service-centre queue using QR/email token access and email alerts.

Waiting-Time Research

Whitt (1999), Ibrahim & Whitt (2011) and Ibrahim & Whitt (2018) show how queue state and recent service history can support delay estimates.

Service Workflow Research

Çil (2021) studies customer behaviour and service operations in managed queues; QKlinik (2025) and DineWise (2025) demonstrate web / QR-based queue and order workflows in other service settings.

Research Gap & SmartPrint Contribution

These studies address queueing, service status or online ordering separately. SmartPrint combines document submission, print-option selection, price calculation, payment status, token tracking and collection notification for a campus print shop.
